## Build an RFQ That Makes Low-MOQ Quotes Comparable

Small-run quotes are comparable only when every supplier prices the same statement of work. Fix construction — base grammage, coated side, format and dimensions — then print specification: color count, system, print side, pattern and artwork position. Require a digital proof and a physical printed sample before production, because adhesion on a coated release surface cannot be judged from a PDF. Ask each supplier to state its minimum in the same unit and confirm that it applies to the exact size, artwork and packing requested. Buyers comparing programs can compare against our [silicone-coated parchment sheets and rolls](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) program.

## FAQ

### Is this a ranking of the best custom printed parchment paper companies?

No. It is a candidate list assembled from publicly published product pages. The order reflects reading flow, not quality, revenue, market share or audited performance, and no supplier has been audited.

### A supplier page says "no minimum" — can I rely on that?

Treat page-level claims as marketing copy until confirmed. Ask the supplier to restate the minimum for your size, artwork, color count and packing, and to name the unit. Resolve conflicting page claims first.

### What makes low-MOQ quotes comparable?

A shared statement of work: identical construction, print side, color count, proofing gates and packing, with minimums in the same unit. A quote omitting construction or document scope is undocumented.

### Do published minimums guarantee price or lead time?

No. Prices, minimums and lead times are quote-specific. Ask for written unit price, minimum, proofing steps and delivery timing, then keep the approved sample as the repeat-order reference.
