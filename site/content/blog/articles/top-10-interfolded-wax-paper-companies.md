## Introduction

Interfolded dry wax deli paper combines waxed food-handling sheets with one-at-a-time dispensing. Buyers must compare sheet coverage, wax construction, fold quality and pack count, not just price. This shortlist of interfolded wax paper companies starts with those requirements. For configurable sizes and private-label packing, review LANGMAI's [interfolded dry wax deli paper](/products/interfolded-deli-paper/) specification.

These are candidates for qualification, not objectively verified winners. Wax paper is primarily for wrapping, handling and separation, not automatic oven use. Evaluate [custom printed baking parchment paper](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) separately for baking applications.

## RFQ Comparison Checklist for Foodservice Buyers

Send every supplier identical sheet dimensions, paper and wax construction, print coverage, food use, dispenser count, carton requirements and destination market. Separate stock products from custom converting proposals so responsibilities remain clear.

| RFQ field | What to compare |
|---|---|
| Sheet and fold | Dimensions, tolerance, interfold pattern and dispensing |
| Construction | Base-paper weight, wax type, coating weight and coated sides |
| Food use | Menu item, moisture, grease, temperature and contact time |
| Packaging | Sheets per box, boxes per case, labels and carton strength |
| Evidence | SKU-specific food-contact documents and claim support |
| Approval | Production-style sample, trial results and change control |

Run a service trial with the actual food. Check repeated dispensing, tearing, transfer, coverage and odor, then retain the accepted sample and packing specification for repeat-order comparison.

## FAQ

### Does the numbering show which company has the best product?

No. It organizes a research shortlist, not a performance or market-share ranking. Select candidates by application, documentation, samples and commercial fit.

### What makes interfolded wax paper different from flat sheets?

Pulling one interfolded sheet presents the next in a pop-up dispenser; flat sheets lack that sequential fold. Test dispensing reliability before standardizing.

### Can dry wax deli paper be used for oven baking?

Do not assume so. For oven applications, review [silicone-coated parchment sheets and rolls](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) against the required temperature and food process.

### What documents should accompany a supplier quotation?

Request specifications, pack details and food-contact evidence matched to the finished SKU and destination market. Confirm that printed or coated constructions fall within the document scope.
