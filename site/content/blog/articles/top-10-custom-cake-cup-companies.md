## Introduction

Custom cake cups must survive the oven, release cleanly, meet food-contact requirements and carry the brand. For private-label programs, the manufacturer choice is one of the highest-leverage packaging decisions. This shortlist covers ten custom cake cup companies whose official sites document custom printed or customizable cake cups, cupcake liners or paper baking cups. It is a working shortlist, not a performance ranking.

Evidence separates these companies from search noise: each publishes a product or capability page showing custom printing, sizing or design, rather than selling finished cakes, plastic cups or party assortments. Where a supplier publishes no number, the entry says so. Buyers wanting a specification baseline first can review LANGMAI's [custom cake cups and cupcake liner programme](/products/cake-cups/), which documents sizes, customization scope and sampling workflow.

## How We Selected the Companies

The test was narrow: a company qualified only if its own website shows custom printed or customizable cake cups, cupcake liners, tulip cups or paper baking cups. We excluded finished-cake sellers, plastic cup suppliers, party retailers and directory platforms. The list spans China, the Netherlands, Italy, the UK and the US.

Each entry covers the product match, the scenarios it fits, and the questions to verify before shortlisting. Print methods, materials and certifications are attributed to the source page, not standardized. The [factory and certificate library](/factory-certificates/) lists the third-party audits and test reports behind these claims.

## Top 10 Custom Cake Cup Companies for Private-Label Bakery Programs

Each entry states only what official pages support; use them as a long list, not a final ranking.

### 1. LANGMAI (China)

LANGMAI develops food-contact paper cake cups and cupcake liners for private-label bakery programs. Its product page documents 11 reference sizes from 55 to 150 mm top diameter, custom dimensions, logo and pattern development with proofing before mass production, and bulk or private-label retail packing. Verify paper, print system and documents against your bake and market. Source: [LANGMAI — Custom Cake Cups](https://www.lmcakecup.com/products/cake-cups/).

### 2. Tielman (Netherlands)

Tielman has made baking cups since the 1930s and publishes over 100 sizes from 20 to 210 mm, paper qualities from 40 to 75 gsm, up to four print colors and natural, single-color, patterned or branded designs. It fits programs needing a specific size-and-quality match. Verify paper quality against your batter, print process and volume tiers. Source: [Tielman — Baking Cups](https://www.tielman.com/products/baking-cups).

### 3. Novacart (Italy)

Novacart's PBA series covers paper baking cups resistant to oven baking up to 220°C, freezing and food fats, with a self-supporting rolled edge. The page states FSC certified paper and custom-made versions on request. It fits industrial producers and confectioners needing oven-to-shelf presentation. Verify customization scope and automatic-line compatibility. Source: [Novacart — PBA Series](https://novacart.com/en/pba-series).

### 4. Doric Crimped (United Kingdom)

Doric Crimped is a UK manufacturer and private-label supplier of custom greaseproof baking cases. Its site documents plain, printed and foil cases; single, spot or CMYK printing as random or registered designs; water-based food-safe ink; own-brand retail packs; and BRCGS Grade AA, FSC Chain of Custody and Sedex membership. Verify print method against volume. Source: [Doric Crimped — Baking cases and liners](https://www.doriccrimped.co.uk/).

### 5. Hoffmaster (United States)

Hoffmaster's Cooking & Baking category covers pan liners, parchment and bake cups, with published fluted baking cup products. Its custom print page states nearly everything it makes can be custom printed, including digital short-run capability. It fits North American foodservice groups wanting printed bake cups alongside napkins and liners. Verify which SKUs accept custom print and the minimums. Sources: [Hoffmaster — Fluted Baking Cups](https://www.hoffmaster.com/products/6-in-white-fluted-baking-cups-10000-ct) and [Hoffmaster — Custom Print](https://hoffmaster.com?p=14377/).

### 6. House of Marie (Netherlands)

House of Marie supplies bakeries, wholesalers and home-baking retailers using Western European paper. Its baking cups use food-safe ink and the site states FDA and European safety standards, ISEGA certified. It offers baking cups in any size, shape, colour and design or printed with a company logo. Verify custom print minimums and lead times. Source: [House of Marie — About House of Marie](https://houseofmarie.nl/en/about-house-of-marie).

### 7. BOSSAI (China)

BOSSAI manufactures and exports floret and tulip baking cups and muffin liners from food-grade greaseproof paper rated to 220°C. Its site states OEM/ODM support for size, material, structure, printing and finishing, with FDA, LFGB and FSC referenced. It fits distributors and boutique bakeries wanting customizable structure and print. Verify print method, colour limits and market documents. Sources: [BOSSAI — About Us](https://bossaitop.com/about-us/) and [BOSSAI — Muffin Baking Liners](https://bossaitop.com/product-category/baking-liners/muffin-baking-liners/).

### 8. Jiawang (China)

Jiawang operates a factory founded in 2011 producing cupcake and muffin baking cups from oven-safe greaseproof material rated to 220°C, with flexo printing and OEM/ODM service. Its page lists 160–210 gsm white board, a published MOQ of 100,000 pieces per design, about 30 days production after sample confirmation and listed certifications. Verify board versus greaseproof paper for your bake. Source: [Jiawang — Cupcake Cases](https://www.jwcup.com/new-arrival-china-cupcake-cases-customized-high-quality-disposable-cupcake-muffin-baking-cup-for-bakery-jiawang-product/).

Verify the no-minimum claim for your quantity and finish durability. Source: [Wax Papers — Custom Cupcake Liners](https://waxpapers.net/custom-cupcake-liners/).

## Comparing Suppliers and Building the RFQ

With ten candidates, the next step is comparison, not contact. Score product match (cup type, size range, paper or board grade, coating), print capability (colour count, process, random pattern versus registered placement), program structure (MOQ, sample policy, proofing workflow) and documentation (food-contact declarations for the finished printed cup). Turn that into one RFQ: dimensions or dieline request, artwork with Pantone references, bake conditions, packing, destination and volume. Send it to three to five suppliers and require a physical pre-production sample. Cup programmes usually ship alongside pan lining; our [custom printed baking parchment paper](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) specs cover the sheets and rolls that pair with them.

## FAQ

### Q: What exactly are custom cake cup companies?

A: Manufacturers and converters producing paper baking cups, cupcake liners or tulip cups with a buyer's artwork or logo, not finished cakes, plastic cups, party assortments or directory platforms.

### Q: How do I verify a custom cake cup supplier before ordering?

A: Start from the official page — cup types, sizes, paper grade, print method and customization scope. Then request a physical pre-production sample, dieline and food-contact documentation for the finished printed cup in your market.

### Q: What MOQ should I expect for printed cake cups?

A: There is no universal number. MOQ depends on cup size, colour count, coverage, print method and packing. The list includes one published 100,000-piece design MOQ and one advertised no-minimum claim; ask every supplier to state MOQ per design and size in writing.

### Q: Can one supplier cover both cake cups and parchment paper?

A: Some can. LANGMAI quotes cups and pan lining together, so a [private-label parchment paper supplier](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) in both categories becomes one documentation relationship.
