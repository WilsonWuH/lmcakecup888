## Introduction

Packaging converters comparing water-based coated paper companies need more than brand names. Suppliers coat flexible paper, cupstock, folding boxboard and base paper for different processes. This guide shortlists ten companies with official evidence of water-based, waterborne or aqueous dispersion barrier coatings, then outlines procurement fits and trial questions. LANGMAI appears first for its converter audience; the remaining order is not a performance ranking. Its [water-based coated base paper](/products/water-based-coated-paper/) is for downstream converting, not finished cups, bowls, containers or bags.

## How We Selected the Companies

We reviewed official product, technology and mill pages, not directories or aggregators. Selection required:

- **Product match:** water-based, waterborne or aqueous dispersion barrier coating.
- **Converter relevance:** coated base paper, flexible packaging paper or packaging board.
- **Evidence quality:** a specific official product or capability page.
- **Qualification value:** a distinct application or converting route.

This desk-research shortlist is not an audited ranking by quality, revenue or market share. Inclusion does not confirm availability or make a company’s whole portfolio water-based. Buyers sourcing adjacent bakery formats can review our [custom printed baking parchment paper](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) separately; release-coated baking paper needs a different specification.

## Top 10 Water-Based Coated Paper Companies

### 1. LANGMAI

LANGMAI supplies water-based coated base paper for downstream cup, bowl, food-container and paper-bag converting, not finished packages. Listed options span 60–300 gsm by application, custom roll widths, typical ±2 g/m² tolerance and 200 m samples by specification. It suits converters needing construction review before trials. Confirm coating side, food-contact use, sealing route, documents and order terms. [Official product page](https://www.lmcakecup.com/products/water-based-coated-paper/).

### 2. Stora Enso

Stora Enso’s Aqua range includes Performa Natura Aqua folding boxboard and CKB Nude Aqua kraft-back board. Official pages describe water-based dispersion for liquid and grease resistance and identify foodservice, bakery, fast-food and selected dry or frozen uses. These candidates suit printed board converting. Ask which grade, coating side, sealing window and forming conditions match the pack. [Aqua products](https://www.storaenso.com/en/products/paperboard-materials/barrier-boards/new-barrier-solutions) and [coating evidence](https://storaenso.com/en/products/paperboard-materials/barrier-coatings).

### 3. Lecta

Lecta’s EraCup Natural is solid bleached board with an aqueous-dispersion inner side. Lecta identifies flexographic and offset printing, heat sealing and conversion into cups and foodservice packaging. It suits cup and container converters evaluating sealable board, not buyers seeking finished containers. Confirm current grammage, sealing settings, forming performance and documents for the exact food-contact use. [Official EraCup Natural evidence](https://www.lecta.com/en/news/Pages/Lecta-s-EraCup-Natural-achieves-the-flustix-Less-Plastics-Product-certification-driving-sustainable-innovation-in-food-service-packaging.aspx).

### 4. Mitsubishi Paper Mills

Mitsubishi Paper Mills describes barricote as packaging paper with a water-based coating, oxygen and water-vapour barriers, and heat-sealability. Its official article distinguishes barricote from film-laminated barrisherpa, so the constructions are not interchangeable. Barricote merits investigation for flexible food, household or sanitary packaging. Confirm the grade, supply route, sealing conditions and shelf-life testing. [Official barricote article](https://www.mitsubishi.com/en/csr/mpac/monitor/worldscope/202308/3.html).

### 5. Walki

Walki EVO Seal uses water-based dispersion on paper or board for grease, moisture and water-vapour protection. Its page identifies heat-sealable, high-speed converting and flexographic printing for tea envelopes, frozen foods, confectionery and secondary packaging. Request the proposed substrate, then test seal strength, print adhesion, forming and barrier performance with the actual contents. [Official Walki EVO Seal page](https://www.walki.com/products/dispersion-coating-recyclable-medium-barrier-paper-based-solutions/).

### 6. Nippon Paper Industries

Nippon Paper’s SHIELDPLUS applies a barrier layer based on water-based coating technology to wood-derived base paper. The page identifies oxygen and vapour barriers for bags, wrapping and flexible packaging. Standard is listed as a grade; Premier is expressly under development. Confirm commercial availability, sealant or lamination needs, converting conditions and test data for the intended humidity and shelf life. [Official SHIELDPLUS page](https://www.nipponpapergroup.com/english/products/shieldplus/).

### 7. Koehler Paper

Koehler NexPlus Seal Pure MOB is heat-sealable flexible packaging paper with a mineral-oil barrier. An official article identifies a 72 gsm example with a 6 gsm water-based dispersion sealing coating; do not generalize those figures. The product suits dry-food primary and secondary packaging. Request the current specification and trial the proposed reel on the intended forming, printing and sealing line. [Product details](https://www.koehlerpaper.com/en/products/flexible-packaging-paper/FlexTech/NexPlus-Seal-Pure-Mob.php) and [coating evidence](https://www.koehlerpaper.com/en/news/newsletter-flextech/2025-q4-1/Koehler-Knowledge_EPR-Costs.php).

### 8. Mondi

Mondi’s re/cycle FunctionalBarrier Paper range serves HFFS and VFFS packaging, but the official page lists extrusion, aqueous and other barrier technologies. FunctionalBarrier Paper Select specifically uses dispersion coatings. It suits tailored flexible-packaging development; do not assume every item is aqueous-coated. Have Mondi identify the exact construction, coating, sealing route, print process and documents. [Official FunctionalBarrier Paper page](https://www.mondigroup.com/en/products-and-solutions/barrier-coatings).

### 9. Sappi

Sappi’s Guard range includes integrated barrier papers and selected heat-sealable products for pouches, flow-wraps, bags and liners. Its guidance describes paper barrier solutions coated with thin waterborne polymer dispersions. Start with the required oxygen, moisture, grease or mineral-oil barrier. Confirm the quoted Guard grade’s coating construction and request material-specific evidence. [Guard range](https://www.sappi.com/en-fr/products-services/products/guard?language=en-gb) and [waterborne coating explanation](https://www.sappi.com/fi-jp/insights/campaigns/sustainable-tea-packaging).

### 10. MM Kotkamills

MM Kotkamills states that its lightweight virgin-fibre folding boxboard portfolio includes grades with water-based dispersion barriers against moisture and grease. The mill serves foodservice and other demanding cartonboard applications, making it a candidate for board converters. Since the capability page names no individual barrier grades, request current product names, grammage and coating details, then qualify printing, forming, sealing and food-contact documents. [Official capability page](https://mm.group/locations/mm-kotkamills).

## Compare Suppliers Through One RFQ

Compare material and trial requirements before price. “Water-based” alone defines neither dried chemistry nor performance.

| RFQ field | What to specify | Qualification reason |
|---|---|---|
| Application | Contents, contact time, temperature and market | Defines use and evidence |
| Construction | Substrate, grammage, coating side and dried coating | Aligns grade comparisons |
| Format | Reel or sheet size, core and tolerances | Checks line fit |
| Converting | Printing, forming, sealing and machine settings | Makes trials repeatable |
| Evidence | Food-contact scope and support for each claim | Matches documents to material |
| Commercial terms | Trial quantity, MOQ, lead time and packing | Normalizes quotations |

Retain an approved sample and record substrate, coating, ink or adhesive changes. Procurement teams consolidating adjacent paper categories can review [silicone-coated parchment sheets and rolls](/products/baking-parchment-paper/) separately without mixing release-paper and barrier-paper specifications.

## FAQ

### What does water-based coated paper mean?

It describes a coating applied from a water-based formulation or dispersion. The term does not identify the dried chemistry or performance. Request the construction, coating side, barrier target and test conditions.

### Is water-based coated paper automatically plastic-free, recyclable or compostable?

No. These claims require evidence for the exact material or finished package. Local collection and processing conditions also matter. Never infer them from the water-based process label alone.

### Are all products from these ten companies water-based coated?

No. Several companies also supply PE-coated, laminated or other barrier materials. Confirm the coating technology on every quotation and specification rather than extending this shortlist to a whole portfolio.

### What should a converter test before a volume order?

Test the proposed material on the intended line. Record printing, slitting, curling, forming, sealing and stability, then evaluate the converted pack with actual contents. Retain settings, acceptance criteria and a reference sample.
