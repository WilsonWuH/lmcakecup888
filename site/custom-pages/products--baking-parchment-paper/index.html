FAQ

Buyer Questions

What is the MOQ for custom parchment paper?
Minimum order depends on the format, size, printing and packaging configuration. Send your target size and quantity, and we confirm the MOQ together with a quote-ready specification during order review.
Can you make custom sizes and die-cut shapes?
Yes. Sheets, rolls, rounds and custom die-cut shapes are produced to your dimensions. Send a target size, product photo or drawing and we confirm the specification before sampling.
Can LANGMAI print my logo or design?
Yes. Custom colors, sizes, stock designs and OEM artwork are supported with in-house flexographic printing. Artwork, material and carton quantity are confirmed before mass production.
Is registered printing available?
Yes, registered printing is available for constructions that require the print to align with cutting, die-cutting or finished format. Print registration requirements are reviewed at the artwork stage.
How do samples work?
After artwork confirmation we produce a pre-production sample. Mass production starts only after you approve the sample and packaging. Request samples here.
What is the lead time?
Lead time is confirmed per order once the sample is approved and depends on quantity, printing and packaging. Your confirmed schedule is stated in the order review before production starts.
Which food-contact documents can you provide?
The website shows BSCI, ISO, FSC, FDA, LFGB and BRC names or document previews. EUDR traceability is handled separately because it is a due-diligence system rather than a product certificate. Final scope, validity, applicability and report numbers are checked during order review.
Do you offer PFAS-free options?
Yes. LANGMAI can discuss intentionally added PFAS-free material options and provide available supplier declarations or product-specific test evidence for buyer review. See the PFAS-Free Program.
What packaging options are available?
Retail box, poly bag, dispenser box, bulk carton and private label. Export-ready packing with lot identification is standard for shipment preparation.
What artwork files do you need?
Vector artwork (AI / PDF / EPS) is preferred for logo and pattern printing. If you only have a reference product or a photo, send it — we review feasibility and artwork requirements before sampling.
